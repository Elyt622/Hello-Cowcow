# Hello CowCow 2026 — Product direction

This branch translates the approved dark mockup into the real Android app without replacing the CowCow identity.

## Principles

- Keep CowCow red and MOOVE yellow as strong accents.
- Use layered charcoal surfaces rather than flat crypto-black.
- Let NFT artwork dominate cards and detail pages.
- Prefer dense, scannable market information over decorative cards.
- Keep public exploration wallet-free.
- Make xPortal actions explicit and user-confirmed.
- Treat Recovery as a guided safety flow, never as a hidden transaction shortcut.
- Adapt navigation and grids to phone, tablet and large screens.

## Target surfaces

1. Explore — public ecosystem dashboard.
2. Collection — market and collection intelligence.
3. NFT detail — artwork, rank, status, traits and offers.
4. Portfolio — wallet identity, rewards, owned/staked/listed CowCows.
5. Recovery — MOOVE acquisition, funding, unstake, unbond and optional swap back.

## Product rule

No visual polish may weaken transaction clarity. A transaction that moves assets must always show the action, amount and destination before xPortal signing.
